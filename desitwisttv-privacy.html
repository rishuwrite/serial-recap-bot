Privacy Policy

Application: DesiTwistTV Publisher
Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DesiTwistTV Publisher (“the app”) handles information when its operator uses it to publish videos and related content to social media accounts they manage.

Information the app may access

Depending on the permissions granted by the account owner, the app may access basic Instagram account information, Facebook Page information, and authorization needed to publish content. The automation may also process video files, captions, publishing results, and status records needed to carry out and troubleshoot publishing.

How information is used

Information is used to authorize connected accounts, publish content at the operator’s request, check publishing status, and prevent duplicate publishing attempts. The app is not intended to sell personal information or use it for advertising profiling.

Storage and sharing

Authentication tokens and app credentials should be stored in restricted secrets or other secure storage. Publishing status records may be stored for operational troubleshooting and duplicate prevention. Information is not sold. Data may be processed by Meta and by hosting or automation providers used to run the app, subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Publishing records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for automation, troubleshooting, and record keeping. The account owner can revoke the app’s access through Meta’s settings. To request deletion of information held by the app operator, contact the email listed below and identify the relevant account or data. Some records may remain where retention is necessary for security or legal obligations.

Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect access tokens, app secrets, and publishing records. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

This app is an account-publishing utility and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s functionality or data practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
